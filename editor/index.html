        HOW IT WORKS

          ☰ holds the set: its holes in order, their names and par, and saving. The pill beside it says whether the set is ready, and tapping it lists what to fix.

          Everything snaps to the grid; hold Shift to place freely. Undo is the arrow under the tools, or ⌘Z / Ctrl Z. The dashed box is the size limit.

          On a computer, right-click or Delete removes what's under the pointer; Enter closes a shape and Esc cancels it.
